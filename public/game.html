2 The Two Trigger Rings (Which Hand Hits Which Ring?)
Outer Ring (Cyan)
Triggered by: LONG MINUTE HAND

12 outer sockets (0m..55m). Shifts time by minutes (+20m, -15m, +10m).

Inner Ring (Amber)
Triggered by: SHORT HOUR HAND

12 inner sockets (1h..12h). Shifts time by hours (+4h, -3h, +2h).
