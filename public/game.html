CHRONOS: Field Guide & Tutorial

Simple Rules to Master the Clock

1 The Goal: Hit the Target Time

The clock runs for a set duration (e.g. 75 minutes). Without any pins, natural time will land somewhere (e.g. 01:00 + 75m = 02:15). Your mission is to slot time-warping pins to make the clock finish on the exact Target Time (e.g. 02:35)!

01:00 Start + 75m Run = 02:15 ➔ Target: 02:35 +20m Needed!
2 The Two Trigger Rings
Outer Ring (Cyan: 0m..55m)

Triggered by the Minute Hand as it sweeps across the 5-minute ticks.

Inner Ring (Amber: 1h..12h)

Triggered by the Hour Hand as it sweeps past Roman numerals.

3 The Time-Warping Pins
+20m Forward Advances clock 20 min
-15m Backward Rewinds clock 15 min
+10m Forward Advances clock 10 min
4 How to Place Pins (Super Simple!)
  • 👉 Tap-to-Place (Easiest): Tap a pin in your tray, then tap any socket on the clock!
  • 👉 Drag-and-Drop: Or drag any pin directly into a clock socket.
  • 👉 Remove: Tap any slotted pin on the clock to return it to your tray.
